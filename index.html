About BTS-

BTS is a seven member south korean boy band k-pop group. They formed in 2010 and debuted in 2013.

The seven members:

  • RM (Kim Namjoon)-Leader and rapper
  • Jin (Kim Seokjin)-Vocalist
  • Suga (Min Yoongi)-Rapper and producer
  • J-Hope(Jung Hoseok)-Rapper and dancer
  • Jimin (Park Jimin)-Vocalist and dancer
  • V (Kim Taehyung)-Vocalist
  • Jung Kook (Jeon Jungkook)-Main vocalist

Music Style and Achievements

  • Genres:Started as hip-hop group before expanding into pop, R&B, EDM, and rock
  • Lyrics:Frequently explore themes of mental health, youth, self-love, and social pressures
  • Global Fandom:Known as ARMY, a massive international fan base that supports their music and efforts

BTS is one of the most popular k-pop groups world wide. There songs get lots of views and they have millions of fans.
