Random facts about BTS!

Name Origin: BTS stands for Bangtan Sonyeodan (Bulletproof Boy Scouts) and the group later announced it also means Beyond the scene

Genres: BTS originally started as hip-hop group before expanding into pop, R&B, EDM, and rock

Lyrics: The lyrics in BTS will frequently involve themes of mental health, youth, slef love, and social pressures

Fandom: BTS fans are known as ARMY, which stands for Adorable representative M.C for youth.

Military Service: All seven members completed their mandatory South Korean national service by mid-2025 ahead of their group activities.

First Member: RM was the first trainee recruited into the lineup that eventually became BTS.

Global Outreach: In 2018, BTS deliverd a speech at the United Nations General Assembly to launch a UNICEF youth education campaign.
